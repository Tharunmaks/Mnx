# History

## Events

World War I lasted from 1914 to 1918.
World War II lasted from 1939 to 1945.
The United States Declaration of Independence was adopted on July 4, 1776.
The French Revolution began in 1789 with the storming of the Bastille.
The Berlin Wall fell on November 9, 1989, and Germany reunified in 1990.
Apollo 11 landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969; Neil Armstrong was the first person to walk on the Moon, followed by Buzz Aldrin.
Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space on April 12, 1961.
Johannes Gutenberg introduced movable-type printing in Europe around 1440.
Christopher Columbus first reached the Americas in 1492.
The Roman Empire in the West fell in 476 AD.
The Magna Carta was sealed in England in 1215.
India became independent from British rule on August 15, 1947.
The United Nations was founded in 1945.
The Soviet Union dissolved in December 1991.

## People

Mahatma Gandhi led India's non-violent independence movement.
Nelson Mandela became South Africa's first Black president in 1994.
Martin Luther King Jr. gave the "I Have a Dream" speech in 1963.
Marie Curie was the first person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences, physics (1903) and chemistry (1911).
Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa.
William Shakespeare wrote Hamlet, Macbeth and Romeo and Juliet.
Ada Lovelace wrote what is often called the first computer program, for Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine, in 1843.
Alan Turing described the Turing machine in 1936 and helped break the Enigma code during World War II.
