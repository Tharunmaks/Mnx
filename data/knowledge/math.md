# Mathematics

Pi (π) is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter, approximately 3.14159.
Euler's number e is approximately 2.71828 and is the base of the natural logarithm.
The Pythagorean theorem states that in a right triangle a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse.
A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 whose only divisors are 1 and itself.
There are infinitely many prime numbers, as Euclid proved around 300 BC.
The area of a circle is π times the radius squared (A = πr²).
The circumference of a circle is 2π times the radius.
The sum of the interior angles of a triangle is 180 degrees.
The golden ratio is (1 + √5) / 2, approximately 1.618.
Zero is neither positive nor negative, and it is an even number.
A right angle measures 90 degrees.
The Fibonacci sequence starts 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, and each term is the sum of the two before it.
Fermat's Last Theorem was proved by Andrew Wiles in 1994.
