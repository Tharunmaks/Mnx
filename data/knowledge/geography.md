## Continents and oceans

There are seven continents: Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Australia (Oceania), Europe, North America and South America.
Asia is the largest continent by both area and population.
The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth.
The five oceans are the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern and Arctic oceans.
The deepest known point in the ocean is the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, about 10,900 metres below sea level.

## Landmarks and records

Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level, at about 8,849 metres, on the border of Nepal and China.
The Nile and the Amazon are the two longest rivers in the world; the Nile is usually listed as longest at about 6,650 km, though some measurements favour the Amazon.
The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world; Antarctica is the largest desert overall.
Russia is the largest country by area.
Vatican City is the smallest country by area.
India and China are the two most populous countries, each with more than 1.4 billion people.
The Dead Sea shore is the lowest land point on Earth, more than 400 metres below sea level.
