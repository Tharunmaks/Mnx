      Questions

      What happens when Pro ends?

      Your Bees go back to Free computers. Nothing is deleted: your files, chats and Bees are kept.

      Is my payment safe?

      Payments run on Razorpay's own checkout. The Hive creates the order and checks the payment's signature on the server; your card or UPI details never reach it.

      Does Pro renew automatically?

      No. It lasts 30 days from each payment, and paying again adds 30 more days on top.

      What is the GPU for?

      Pro Bees can run training and heavy jobs on the Hive's 16 GB GPU, for example training a small model from the chat.
