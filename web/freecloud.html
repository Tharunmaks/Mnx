Connecting

Plus is free. Every Bee and bot gets 4 CPUs, 16 GB RAM, 100 GB disk and a GPU with 16 GB VRAM. Users never bring a GPU or a key: the Hive gives them, from the free cloud below. The Hive's owner sets it up once (about 10 minutes, no payment); after that the Hive runs it by itself.

Free GPU · 16 GB VRAM

Kaggle gives every account a free NVIDIA GPU with 16 GB VRAM (T4 or P100), about 30 hours a week. The Hive sends Bees' GPU jobs there and brings the results back into their computers.

  1. Sign up free at kaggle.com and verify your phone number (needed for GPUs).
  2. Open Settings → API and press Generate New Token; copy it.
  3. Paste your Kaggle user name and the token here.

Free server · 24 GB RAM, 200 GB disk

Oracle Cloud's Always Free machine (ARM, 4 CPUs, 24 GB RAM, 200 GB disk) costs nothing, forever. The Hive puts Plus Bees on it: two 16 GB Bees per machine (memory limits are ceilings, so they share it well). Add more machines for more Bees.

  1. Sign up free at oracle.com/cloud/free (a card is asked to check you're real; Always Free isn't charged).
  2. Create a compute instance: image Ubuntu, shape VM.Standard.A1.Flex with 4 OCPUs and 24 GB, boot volume 200 GB.
